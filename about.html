Who We Are

Tanzania's Most Trusted Study Abroad Consultancy

Strate Bridge Consultancy was founded with one clear mission — to make international education accessible to every Tanzanian student with the dream of studying abroad.

Based in Dar es Salaam, we have helped hundreds of students gain admission to top universities across the world. Our team combines deep knowledge of international education systems with a genuine passion for student success.

We believe that every student deserves a chance to reach their full potential — and sometimes that journey begins by crossing borders.

Our Mission
